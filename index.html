FROSTHEAVE

A no-guess road-clearing logic puzzle

How to play

1. Dig. Tap a snowy square. Your first tap is always safe.

2. Read the numbers. A number counts the potholes and frost heaves touching that square, all 8 neighbors.

3. Plant cones. Hold a square until the ring fills (about half a second) to mark a hazard, or switch to Cone mode. A quick tap always digs.

4. Open fast. When a number has all its cones, tap the number to dig the rest around it.

No guessing is ever needed: every road can be cleared by logic alone. Stuck? The lightbulb shows a safe square. Finished numbers fade so you can see what's left.

One slip. The first hazard you dig on a road is forgiven: it gets a cone and costs 20 seconds. The heart by the cone counter shows it is still there. The second one ends the road.

Smart cones. If a number can only mean hazards (it needs exactly as many cones as it has snowy squares), tap it and the cones go down for you.

Storm Shift. Small boards, one clock. You start with 40 seconds and every board opens already started. Clearing a board adds time (the clock holds at most a minute); the bar along the top of the board is your clock. A dug hazard costs 10 seconds, a hint costs 8. How many boards can you plow before the clock runs out?

Pothole Run

You have HP. Digging a hazard costs its strength (1 to 4) in HP, then the hole is filled.

Dig to earn XP and level up. Every hazard you never dig pays XP when the stretch clears. Then pick an upgrade. How far can you plow?
